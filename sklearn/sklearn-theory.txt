The Core Idea

Machine learning is just learning patterns from examples, then using those patterns to make guesses about new things.
Think of it like this: if you've eaten at 20 restaurants and noticed that restaurants with long wait times usually have good food, you've "learned" a pattern. Next time you see a crowded restaurant, you predict the food is probably good.
That's exactly what ML does, just with math.

We can build custom estimator as well

2. transformer - yeh data se sheekne ke baaad new data pe transforamtion krta hai, iske pass .transform and .fit_transform hota hai.

MIXINS
mixins classes are auxiliary classes that provide additional methods and  fucntionality to custoom estimators.
They are dsigned to be used through multiple inhertitance, allowing custom estimatoers to gain standardized capabilities
such as scoring, fiting, predication, or transforming without need of reimlpemeting these methods from sratch


TRANSFORMERS
yeh data se sheekne ke baaad new data pe transforamtion krta hai, iske pass .transform and .fit_transform hota hai. 
It is used for transforming datasets. transformer are designed to pre-process data, which can include tasks suchs as
scaling, encoding categorical variables, handling missing values, and the feature extraction.
